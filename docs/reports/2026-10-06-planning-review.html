Why the Chart of Accounts is empty

Epic SOUPFIN-142 · plans/coa-template-seeding.md

Root cause
  • Registration sends the business type, but the backend never creates any accounts from it.
  • SERVICES companies get 403 on /rest/ledgerAccount/*, so even seeded accounts would not show.
  • There is no account-category seed or API, so the frontend cannot seed accounts itself.
  • Without A/R, A/P and tax system accounts, invoice and bill posting fails.
  • On the frontend, the page can render blank, and the empty state offers no next step.
Fix, as planned
  • COA templates for TRADING and SERVICES, plus a Ghana tax overlay (VAT, NHIL, GETFund, WHT); 60–90 accounts each. 143
  • Seed the accounts and enable the modules at registration. 144
  • applyTemplate with dry-run, plus a template list. 145
  • Backfill existing companies whose COA is empty. 146
  • Template picker, blank-page fallback, account CRUD and clearer 403 messages. 147–151
  • Tests and documentation fixes. 152–153
